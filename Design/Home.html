Why OpsSync

Built around the way BdREN already works.

No retraining required. Every form mirrors the paper version your team already signs — only faster, signed digitally, and printable to A4 the moment it's approved.

Live A4 preview

Every form renders a print-ready document on the right as you type — with signatures, seal, ref number.

BdREN SSO

Single sign-on with your existing BdREN directory account. No new passwords. No new accounts.

English & বাংলা

Full UI toggle, with amount-in-words rendered in both languages on every claim.

Role-aware permissions

Requester, Supervisor, Line Manager, Admin, AM/DM, Finance, Sys Admin — each sees only their relevant queues.

Three-tier dashboards

Personal, Operational, Executive — each role lands on a dashboard tuned to the decisions they need to make.

Live SLAs & escalation

Every stage has a clock. Auto-escalation kicks in at 48h to keep nothing stuck on someone's desk.

Before OpsSync, a single vehicle requisition could lose two days passing through three desks. Now the same request is approved before lunch — and the trip log writes itself.
